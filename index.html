About

A house built for those who define their own.

YOD is a premium streetwear house born in Bangalore, India, and crafted for the ones who refuse to borrow their identity. We believe clothing is not costume — it is declaration. Every piece we make exists to carry someone's definition of themselves out into the world.

Our silhouettes are shaped by construction, not graphics. Heavyweight fabrics. Cuts that hold their line through the day, through the season, through the years. We draft each garment as architecture — mock-necks, drop shoulders, considered proportions — so the piece speaks before any logo does.

YOD stands for Your Own Design. The brand, the garments, and the people who wear them are all here for the same reason: to wear a definition they chose, not one they were handed.

— Made in India. Worn by intention.
Our Story

The ones who define, not the ones who follow.

YOD began with a quiet frustration. Walk any street in India and the shirts tell the same story — borrowed logos, chased trends, the loud sameness of what everyone else was wearing that month. Somewhere in that noise, individuality had gone missing.

We built YOD for the ones who were already going the other way. The quiet rebels. The ones who had stopped asking what was in and started asking what was theirs. The vulture in our mark says it best — patient, unflinching, decisive. It does not follow the flock; it waits, it watches, and then it moves.

Every YOD piece carries that posture. Heavyweight cotton that remembers its shape. Construction you can feel before you can see it. A design language stripped of noise so what remains is you, the person wearing it, and the definition you chose for yourself.

This is not a label you put on. It is a line you draw.
